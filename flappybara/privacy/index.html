Flappybara Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Flappybara ("the game") is made by tmpst ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses and why. We don't run our own servers for the game, we don't ask you to create an account, and we don't sell your personal information.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress on your device: best score, yuzu collected, medals, settings (music and sound on or off), and whether you've bought Remove Ads. This data stays on your device. We don't receive it. Deleting the game deletes it.

Game Center

If you're signed in to Apple Game Center, the game submits your best score to the Flappybara leaderboard. Apple shows your Game Center nickname and score to other players. Apple handles this data under the Apple Privacy Policy. You can sign out of Game Center in iOS Settings at any time.

In-app purchase

The optional "Remove Ads" purchase is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. We only receive confirmation of whether the purchase was made, so the game can turn off ads.

Advertising

If you haven't bought Remove Ads, the game occasionally shows full-screen ads provided by Google AdMob. To deliver ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect information such as:

  • device information (model, operating system version, an app-specific device identifier)
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it
  • ad interactions (for example, whether an ad was shown or tapped) and diagnostic data

The game doesn't request permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites (Apple's App Tracking Transparency), so it doesn't share Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA). The game requests non-personalized ads only, limited to content suitable for all audiences.

Google uses this information as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy. Where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent before ads are requested, and you can change your choice later.

Analytics

The game contains no analytics or tracking SDKs beyond the advertising SDK described above. Apple may give us aggregated, anonymous App Store statistics (for example, number of downloads) if you've chosen to share them with developers in iOS Settings.

Children

The game isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. Ads are limited to content rated for general audiences.

Your choices

  • Buy Remove Ads to stop all ads and the ad SDK's data collection for ads.
  • Limit ad-related data in iOS Settings, under Privacy & Security.
  • Sign out of Game Center to stop score submission.
  • Delete the game to remove all data stored on your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
